Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take reservations?

We operate on a first-come, first-served basis for regular seating. However, we do accept reservations for groups of 8 or more and for private events. Please contact us at least 48 hours in advance.

Can I host a private event?

Yes! Our space is available for private events such as book launches, birthday parties, baby showers, and corporate gatherings. We offer custom catering menus and can accommodate up to 40 guests after hours.

Do you sell gift cards?

Absolutely! Gift cards are available in any amount and can be purchased in-store or by calling us. They can be used for beverages, food, and book purchases.

Is WiFi available?

Yes, we offer free WiFi for all customers. The network name and password are posted at the counter. We kindly ask that laptop users limit their stay to 2 hours during peak times.

Do you buy used books?

We operate a book exchange program! Bring in your gently used books and receive store credit toward your next purchase. We accept most genres in good condition.

Are you pet-friendly?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on our outdoor patio! We even have a water bowl and complimentary dog biscuits. Service animals are welcome throughout the entire cafe.
